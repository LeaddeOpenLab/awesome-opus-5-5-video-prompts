## Prompt (English)

```text
Build "Rubik's Cube", a single self-contained HTML file, three.js r160 only. A glossy, realistic 3x3 Rubik's cube in a dark studio with soft lighting and a subtle shadow or reflection. Standard colors. Layers must really rotate. 0-1.5s solved cube turning, 1.5-5s it scrambles itself with 20 seeded random turns, 5-14s it solves itself with every turn animated (any method allowed, including undoing the scramble), 14-15s a small celebration. Slow cinematic camera, no UI.
```
