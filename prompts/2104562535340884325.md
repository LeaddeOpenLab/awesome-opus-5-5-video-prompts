## Prompt (English)

```text
make a dynamic motion graphics video about [PASTE YOUR WEBSITE HERE] that shows what an incredible motion designer you are, like it's your showreel for a résumé. go all out.

First explore and understand the product, its audience, visual identity and what makes it interesting.

Turn that understanding into an insanely creative motion graphics film.

Keep it as short and concise as possible while still telling a compelling story and showcasing the product properly. Maximum duration: 60 seconds. Never stretch the video just to reach 60 seconds.

Don't make a generic product demo or SaaS explainer.

Don't just animate screenshots.

Use the actual product as inspiration for the story, visuals and animation.

Show what the product does through creative visual metaphors, UI animation, kinetic typography, cinematic camera movement, unexpected transitions, morphing interfaces, 2D and 3D motion and whatever other techniques make the film spectacular.

Use the product's existing branding, website and visual identity as inspiration.

Give yourself complete creative freedom. Don't follow a predictable storyboard. Surprise me.

The product should be understandable, but the main objective is to demonstrate how insanely good your motion design can be.

Actually create the finished video, not a storyboard, concept or explanation.

Make it feel like an award-winning motion designer created a personal showreel piece for this product.

Make every second count.

go absolutely wild.
```
