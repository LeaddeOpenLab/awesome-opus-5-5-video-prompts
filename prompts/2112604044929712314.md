## Prompt (English)

```text
GOAL:
Create a highly detailed, cinematic, interactive 3D scene in Three.js where a futuristic Tesla Roadster launches from a massive rocket launch complex using reaction thrusters. The result should feel like a premium CGI spectacle with realistic materials, detailed geometry, impressive lighting, rich atmospheric effects, and smooth camera choreography.
CORE CONCEPT:
What if Tesla launched the next-generation Roadster like a SpaceX rocket? The car sits on a launch platform, secured by mechanical hold-down clamps. Warning lights flash, vapor builds, thrusters ignite, clamps release, and the Roadster rises into a controlled hover before accelerating into the sky. This is a cinematic concept, not a realistic engineering simulation.

CAR DESIGN:
Create a highly recognizable next-generation Tesla Roadster using the provided reference images. Match its silhouette, proportions, front fascia, headlights, body curves, roofline, wheels, and overall appearance as closely as possible. Use detailed geometry, premium automotive paint, realistic-looking glass, metallic materials, and convincing reflections. Avoid generic sports car shapes or overly simplistic geometry. The Roadster must remain clearly visible throughout the important stages.
LAUNCH COMPLEX:
Build a massive modern rocket launch facility with a tall launch tower, industrial gantry structures, service platforms, mechanical hold-down clamps, support arms, railings, stairs, pipes, floodlights, warning lights, vapor vents, and distant infrastructure. The environment should feel large, believable, and highly detailed. Choose a dramatic coastal, industrial, or desert setting with cinematic atmospheric lighting.

AUTOMATIC ANIMATION:
The entire sequence must start automatically when the page loads, without clicking anything. No intro screen, blank scene, or long waiting period. The first frame must already look impressive, and interesting motion should begin within the first 1–3 seconds.
ANIMATION SEQUENCE:

Hero Reveal: Begin with a cinematic shot of the Roadster on the launch platform. The camera moves smoothly while warning lights flash, vapor drifts, and industrial lighting illuminates the vehicle.
Pre-Launch: Increase atmospheric tension with smoke, pressure vents, mechanical movements, subtle vibration, and gradually intensifying thruster illumination.

Ignition: Activate the thrusters with bright exhaust, expanding smoke, particles, reflections, and lighting effects interacting with the surrounding structures.
Clamp Release: The mechanical hold-down clamps must visibly unlock, rotate, and retract away from the car. Animate their actual 3D components rather than making them disappear.

Hover: The Roadster lifts slightly above the platform and remains suspended briefly. Thrusters continue firing while smoke, dust, and lighting react beneath the vehicle. Make this moment dramatic and clearly visible.

Liftoff: The Roadster accelerates vertically past the launch tower. The camera smoothly follows the car, emphasizing scale, speed, and the power of the propulsion system.

Final Shot: Transition to a wide cinematic view showing the Roadster climbing into the sky, leaving an atmospheric exhaust trail above the launch complex.

TIMELINE:
Aim for an approximately 18–22 second cinematic sequence. Use the first 3 seconds for the hero shot and buildup, seconds 3–5 for ignition and clamp release, seconds 5–8 for hover, seconds 8–15 for ascent, and the remaining time for a dramatic final shot. Synchronize all mechanical movements, lighting changes, particles, vehicle motion, and camera animation. Use time-based animation for smooth playback.

CAMERA & LIGHTING:
Create professional cinematic camera movements, including establishing shots, slow orbits, close-ups, low-angle hero shots, hover tracking, and ascent-following shots. Keep the Roadster readable and avoid chaotic camera movements. Use dramatic sunrise, blue-hour, or nighttime lighting with industrial floodlights, realistic-looking reflections, atmospheric haze, and thruster illumination.

VISUAL EFFECTS:
Include detailed smoke plumes, pressure vapor, exhaust particles, dust, atmospheric mist, glowing thrusters, subtle heat distortion, environmental reflections, and tasteful bloom or post-processing. Effects should feel connected to the action rather than purely decorative. Smoke and lighting must react convincingly to the launch without obscuring the vehicle.

INTERACTION:
Autoplay is the priority, but optional controls may include replay, pause/resume, slow motion, camera switching, and free orbit. Keep the interface minimal and visually clean. The scene must be fully functional without any manual interaction.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
Use Three.js, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. You may use suitable open-source assets, libraries, custom shaders, particle systems, and post-processing to improve quality. Organize the project into multiple files if needed. Optimize for smooth real-time rendering and make sure it runs locally in a modern browser. This must be a real interactive 3D experience, not a prerecorded video. Test the completed scene and fix major visual or functional issues.

IMPORTANT FOR RECORDING:
The scene should be ready to record immediately after refreshing the page. Prioritize a powerful first frame, continuous animation, clearly visible mechanical movements, impressive hover and liftoff moments, and a satisfying cinematic ending. Avoid unnecessary delays or static sections.
```
