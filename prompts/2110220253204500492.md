## Prompt (English)

```text
Build "Gummy Bears" - a single self-contained HTML file (relying only on three.js from CDN): a small handful of translucent gummy bears on a white table that you can grab, stretch, squish, throw, and dump. Gummy Bears - classic gummy bear silhouette: round head with two ears, short muzzle, chubby body, stubby arms and legs, small molded eyes and nose - each part smoothly melting into the next - build body as implicit surfaces (metaballs / marching cubes), not separate spheres - Material: realistic gummy candy. Translucent with transmission and refraction, color light absorption deeper in thick parts, glossy lacquer with sharp highlights - Colors: cherry red, orange, lemon yellow, apple green, clear pineapple - soft colored shadow under each bear, tinted by the bear's own color. Physics (this is the whole point - must feel like jelly, not plastic) - each bear is a soft body: mass points for head, ears, muzzle, body, belly, hands, and feet connected via volume-preserving shape matching - limbs and neck rendered as stretchable chains between points that thin when pulled - grab any part and pull: it stretches like rubber, the rest of the bear tilts along, snaps back with damped wobble on release - pull head upward: neck stretches, body follows, releases into squash and jump - press head downward: bear squashes like a pancake, bulges laterally, springs back up - throw a bear: it flies, squashes on landing, bounces, and shoves other bears - bears collide with each other, never pass through - idle bears do small random hops with squash and stretch - "Pour more bears" drops a handful from above into a wobbly, jiggling pile. UI - editorial product page style, white background. Large italic serif header "Gummy / Bears.", a short subhead, a minimalist card on the right with flavor swatches, a wobble slider, live "Stretch" and "Bounce" readouts, and a "Pour more bears" button - a subtle circular cursor that expands on grab - ACES tone mapping, environment reflections, soft key and rim lighting. Smooth 60fps.
```
