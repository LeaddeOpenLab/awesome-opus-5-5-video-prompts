## Prompt (English)

```text
Make a short video: a hand is drawing a medieval map. Where it writes "here be dragons," the ink starts to move. The goal is to demonstrate eeriness, art direction, material truth, and sound design.

THE STORY (yours to decide)

The first act is pure craft: nib, parchment, a competent hand. Make it satisfying to watch.
The dragon is wet ink. It doesn’t have a body; it has spread, bleed, and intent. It eats towns by smearing them.
The cartographer fights with what’s on the desk: blotter, knife, sand, candle. Decide who wins, and make it a draw if you can defend it.
ART DIRECTION
Not realistic, but materially truthful: iron-gall ink, vellum grain, candle light. Calibration: Mappa Mundi; illuminated manuscript margins; ink-in-water macro photography. Limit palette to parchment, two inks, gold leaf, and one blood-red.

CHOOSE YOUR MEDIUM
The ink needs a real fluid or reaction-diffusion simulation on a 2D surface. make it as a html file, use whatever is needed
```
