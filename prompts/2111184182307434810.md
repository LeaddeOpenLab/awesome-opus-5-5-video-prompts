## Prompt (English)

```text
Create a complete, single-file three.js demo (HTML + JS) that recreates the viral “Tesla Roadster flying over a collapsing bridge” concept, and make it **play automatically like a video**.

**Requirements:**

1. **Auto-play like a video**
   - The entire sequence should start automatically when the page loads (no click required to begin).
   - Cinematic camera path that follows the action smoothly from start to finish.
   - After the sequence ends, show a “Replay” button.

2. **Scene**
   - Scenic bridge over a river with forested hills in the background.
   - Clear daytime lighting with good shadows.

3. **Bridge Collapse**
   - The bridge dramatically collapses in the middle while the car is driving on it.
   - Use either physics (Cannon.js or Rapier) or a well-timed animation sequence.

4. **Car**
   - Simple but recognizable Tesla Roadster / Cybertruck-style car.
   - When the bridge starts collapsing, the car activates thrusters and flies over the gap.
   - Blue thruster glow + particle effects.
   - Smooth flight animation, then lands safely on the other side.

5. **Camera**
   - Fully cinematic, pre-scripted camera that moves for dramatic angles (starts behind the car, then shifts during the flight).
   - No free camera control during the sequence (so it feels like watching a video).

6. **Polish**
   - Clean modern three.js code (r160+).
   - “Replay” button that restarts the full sequence.
   - Responsive and runs smoothly in the browser.
   - Add basic ambient + directional lighting, fog, and a sky.

7. **Output**
   - Provide the full single HTML file I can copy and open directly in a browser.
   - Comment the important parts of the code.

Make it visually impressive and as close as possible to the concept of a flying Tesla saving itself from a collapsing bridge, while playing automatically like a short video.
```
