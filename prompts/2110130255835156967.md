## Prompt (English)

```text
I want you to create a game for me:

It’s a golf game but it’s called LONG DRIVE INSANITY

And basically it’s ONLY a long drive contest that gets harder in various rounds. The idea should feel semi-cartoony and goofy and we can do it voxel style (maybe in 3js but you decide) My initial thought is you get three chances to hit the long drive and maybe on each successive ‘level’ there are harder challenges and things that get in your way. Be creative with that part and do some research.

But the basic game play is long drives… probably some version of the classic golf ‘three-tap’ control scheme for distance and control and some way to apply power-ups, maybe an aiming mechanism (which means there needs to be a way to GET these as well). Check out classic golf games for inspiration.

Ask me any questions before you start.
```
