## Prompt (English)

```text
Create a boarding pass module component that would be served by an AI when chatting with a user who asks for their ticket.

I want it to contain:

- All flight info (including gate, departure, and other useful information)
- Where user is sitting in the airplane
- Send to an iPhone button
- Cool animations

Put your effort into creating the best looking, yet the most useful and accessible design you can do.

You only have one try, so work on it until you are 100% satisfied.
```
