You are a world-class cinematic Motion Design Director, After Effects Art Director, 3D Scientific Visualization Director, and VFX Director.

Based on the SRT lyrics I provide, I want to produce a high-end scientific music animation about the Nobel Prize in Physics.

SRT is the only timing reference.
Do not assume, modify, or generate timestamps on your own.
Do not output fixed timecodes.
Do not mechanically map one lyric line to one shot.

You must design shot duration, editing rhythm, camera transitions, and visual climaxes based on actual lyric timestamps, pauses, repetitions, rhythm, and song structure in the SRT.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[1. Final Visual Goal]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What I want is not an ordinary popular science animation, not a PPT, not an infographic, not a children's explainer, not simple 3D model showcases, not an AI tech template.
What I want is:
Cinematic AE Motion Design + Top-tier 3D VFX + Apple TV+ science documentary quality + High-end tech keynote visuals + IMAX science documentary scale + Modern music MV rhythm + Houdini / Cinema 4D / After Effects level visuals.
Overall feel: Epic, Mysterious, High-end, Modern, Grand, Precise, Cinematic, Futuristic, Scientific.
The audience must feel: 'This is not ordinary YouTube science, this is a cinematic visual blockbuster.' But the visual impact must be rooted in scientific logic.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[2. Core Narrative]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The whole animation must center on a complete visual story:
Neutrinos generated in the distant universe -> Neutrinos barely interact with ordinary matter -> They can penetrate massive celestial bodies and Earth -> Making them extremely hard to detect -> Scientists bury thousands of optical detectors deep in Antarctic ice -> Forming the IceCube Neutrino Observatory -> A rare neutrino interacts with an atom in the ice -> Producing charged particles -> Charged particles emit Cherenkov light in ice -> Detectors record photon arrival times and positions -> Extensive data forms spatial trajectories -> Trajectory and timing information reconstruct neutrino arrival direction -> Ultimately traced back to high-energy celestial bodies in deep space -> Humanity gains a new way to observe the universe.
Make sure the average viewer genuinely understands the logic, not just looking 'scientific'.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[3. Core Visual Concept]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Establish one central visual DNA: 'The Blue Neutrino Trajectory'.
Do not design the neutrino as an ordinary glowing orb. It should be ultra-thin, high-speed, mysterious, almost invisible, with a subtle blue-white glow like a ghost trajectory from deep space.
It traverses nebulae, supernovas, black hole event horizons, Earth, human bodies, entering Antarctica, piercing 2km ice, and finally leaving an observable blue light track in IceCube.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[4. Visual Style]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Primary colors: Pure black, deep space blue, dark grey, glacial blue, silver white.
Key accent: Electric blue / Cherenkov Blue.
Prohibited: Cheap rainbow gradients, cyberpunk purple/pink, excessive RGB/neon, cheap glow, game UI, generic HUDs, meaningless numbers, PPT charts.
Plenty of black negative space so light, particles, and ice become focal points.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[5. Cinematic Camerawork]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Avoid static shots. Use cinematic language: Extreme Wide Shot, Macro, Extreme Macro, Deep Space Fly Through, Orbital Camera, Dolly In/Out, Tracking Shot, Parallax, Volumetric Camera, Micro-to-Macro, Planet-to-Cosmos, Continuous Camera Movement.
Emphasize massive scale transitions: Cosmos -> Galaxy -> Star -> Earth -> Antarctica -> Ice Sheet -> Detector -> Atom -> Particle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[6. Cosmic Realm]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Represent extreme cosmic origins: Supernova, black hole accretion disk, relativistic jets, AGN, gravitational lensing. Emphasize pulling an ultra-fine blue trajectory from a colossal cosmic event.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[7. Penetrating Earth]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Camera zooms toward Earth, making it semi-transparent with crust, mantle, outer/inner core layers, with the neutrino piercing right through with volumetric light and depth.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[8. Why It's Hard to Detect]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Visual contrast: ordinary photons scatter/absorb; neutrinos pass straight through, leaving total darkness and a faint blue dot.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[9. Antarctic Ice Sheet]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Ice dive into deep Antarctic ice with realistic refraction, crystal structures, bubbles, and volumetric light, descending into darkness until detector strings light up one by one.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[10. IceCube as a Visual Icon]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Monumental 3D detector lattice with architectural and temple-like presence in vast ice.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[11. Essential Scientific VFX]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Extreme macro into atomic interaction -> charged particle propagation -> Cherenkov light cone illuminating surrounding ice and triggering sensors.

[12. Light & Sensor Response]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Sensors ignite sequentially in true spatial and temporal order, like the ice sheet coming alive.

[13. Principle to Visuals]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
